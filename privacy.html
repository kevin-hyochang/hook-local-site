The app keeps your data on your machine

Webhook payloads, headers, verifier secrets, replays, and settings are stored in a SQLite file on your computer. None of it is sent to us: there is no hook-local server that could receive it, no account, and no analytics or telemetry. License keys are checked offline against a public key built into the app, so activating one sends nothing.

The app makes an outbound request in only three cases:

  • Once a day it asks the npm registry for the latest version number. No data of yours is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • When you replay an event, it sends that event to the URL you entered.
  • When you start it with --tunnel, deliveries to the public URL travel through Cloudflare's network on their way to your machine.

When you buy Pro

Payment is handled by Gumroad. Your card details go to Gumroad and its payment processors; we never see them. Gumroad's own use of your information is described in the Gumroad privacy policy.

Gumroad passes us your email address and the details of your order (product, price, date, and country). We use them to send your license key, to answer support requests, and to reissue a lost key. For that last purpose we keep a record of each key issued (email, date, key) on our own computer, not on a server, for as long as you might need the key reissued. You can ask us to delete it, after which we can no longer reissue your key.

When you email us

We use your address and message only to reply to you.
